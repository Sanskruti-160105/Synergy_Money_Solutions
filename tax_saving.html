What is Tax Planning?

Tax planning is not simply about finding an investment that saves tax. It is about understanding your income, expenses, investments and financial goals together, and then structuring your finances in a tax-efficient manner.

A well-planned approach can help you: Reduce avoidable tax outgo Improve your effective take-home income Choose investments based on both financial goals and tax implications Plan your cash flows more efficiently Build wealth while remaining tax-compliant Avoid last-minute investment decisions just to save tax

The right approach is to first understand your overall financial position, evaluate the applicable tax regime and then make investment decisions accordingly.
