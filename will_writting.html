What is Will Writing?

A will is a legal document through which an individual can state how they would like their assets and property to be distributed after their death, subject to applicable laws and legal requirements.

It allows an individual to communicate their wishes regarding beneficiaries and the distribution of their assets.

Proper estate planning can help provide greater clarity regarding an individual's wishes and financial arrangements for their family and beneficiaries.
TYPES OF WILL WRITTING
 Estate Distribution , Family Protection, Conflict Prevention, Executor Appointment
