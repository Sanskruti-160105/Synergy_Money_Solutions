Fixed Deposit Options

Types of Fixed Deposits

Different types of fixed deposits are available based on the depositor's requirements, tenure and financial goals.

🏦

Bank Fixed Deposit

Bank fixed deposits allow individuals to deposit a specific amount with a bank for a predetermined period at an applicable interest rate.

🏢

Corporate Fixed Deposit

Corporate fixed deposits are offered by eligible companies or financial institutions and may provide different interest rates and tenure options, subject to the terms of the issuer.

👴

Senior Citizen Fixed Deposit

Some banks and financial institutions offer specific fixed deposit schemes for senior citizens, which may provide additional interest benefits subject to applicable terms.

💰

Tax-Saving Fixed Deposit

Tax-saving fixed deposits are specific deposit products that may provide eligible tax benefits subject to applicable tax laws and conditions.
