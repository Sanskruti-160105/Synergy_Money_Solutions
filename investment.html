What is Investment?

Investment involves allocating money to different financial or other assets with the aim of achieving specific financial goals over time.

Different investment options have different levels of risk, return potential, liquidity and time horizons.

Understanding your financial goals, risk tolerance and investment horizon can help in making informed investment decisions.

Explore Mutual Funds

Types Of Mutual funds

Different investment options can serve different financial goals, time horizons and risk preferences.

Equity Funds

Equity Funds invest primarily in company shares. They offer higher growth potential but can have higher market risk. Their goal is to grow your investment by participating in the performance of businesses across sectors. While equity funds can offer higher long-term returns, they also come with higher risk due to market fluctuations.

Debt fund

Invest mainly in fixed-income securities such as bonds and government securities. They generally focus on income and relatively lower volatility than equity funds. These are ideal for short- to medium-term goals or conservative investors.

Hybrid fund

Hybrid fund Invest in a combination of equity and debt making them ideal for investors who want equity exposure but with some protection from market swings. They aim to balance growth potential and stability.
